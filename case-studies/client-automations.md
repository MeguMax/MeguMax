# Client CRM integrations and automation

My client work includes connecting websites and business workflows to GoHighLevel. These examples combine platform configuration with APIs, webhooks and custom interface code.

## Notion to GoHighLevel contact synchronization

I built a Make scenario that transfers contact data from Notion into GoHighLevel. The integration maps fields between the two systems, checks for an existing contact before creating a record, and updates matching contacts. Webhook triggers, error logging and notifications support the workflow. The published case describes this direction of synchronization; a reverse flow was listed as a possible extension.

## Payments and onboarding

Stripe payments trigger CRM updates and onboarding or access emails through GoHighLevel, Zapier and webhooks. The work connects checkout events to contact tagging, workflow triggers and follow-up sequences. Related projects include course access delivery and CRM payment integration for a B2B service.

## Booking and calendar workflows

I built booking pages and forms connected to GoHighLevel CRM records, with confirmations, reminders and follow-up workflows. Vacation-rental projects synchronized calendar availability through the Google Calendar API, Zapier and webhooks. Custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript supported responsive pages and additional form behavior.

## White-label CRM interfaces

I customized GoHighLevel interfaces with JavaScript and CSS to improve navigation, layout and the consistency of the client-facing experience. A public work-history review credits **YOUR R&D and Artem Serdiuk** jointly for GoHighLevel customization, JavaScript/CSS and UI/UX work.
